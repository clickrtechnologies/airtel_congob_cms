CMS CRBT Portal

Transforming the Caller Ring Back Tone Ecosystem

A centralized platform that connects Content Providers, Artists, Mobile Network Operators, and Admins into one intelligent workflow for seamless CRBT content management.

🎵 Platform Features

  • Content Upload Upload songs, tones, and media files with rich metadata and categorization.
  • Approval Workflow Enable real-time approvals and review cycles between Artists, CPs, and MNOs.
  • Contract Management Track contracts, version history, renewals, and expiry notifications.
  • Analytics & Reports Generate reports for subscriptions, RBT downloads, IVR usage, and revenue.
  • Secure Role Access Provide separate dashboards and permissions for Admin, Artist, CP, and MNO users.

🚀 Our Mission

To digitize, simplify, and modernize CRBT licensing and distribution while creating a collaborative ecosystem for the music and telecom industries.

Delivering faster approvals, greater transparency, and better experiences for every stakeholder.

🤝 Benefits to Stakeholders

Content Providers

Manage uploads, monitor approvals, and track performance in one place.

Artists

Stay in control of your songs, rights, and contract lifecycle.

MNOs

Access approved content quickly and simplify reporting and integration.

Admins

Oversee, manage, and audit all portal activities efficiently.
